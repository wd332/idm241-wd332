Triggers

Exhibition Card Underneath Banner:

  • hover on
  • hover off
  • click on

On View Box:

  • hover on
  • hover off

Page Indicators:

  • hover on
  • hover off
  • click on

Rules

Exhibition Card:

  • Hover On
    • expands image from the center slightly by 15%
    • cursor changes to the finger
    • a white drop shadow
    • ease in with a duration of 530ms
      • left & right arrows
      • page indicators
      • a glow of white, 25% opacity drop shadow with a blur of 100 and spread of 10 at origin surrounds the border of the card
    • left & right arrows ease in with a duration of 530ms
    • page indicators ease in with a duration of 530ms
  • Hover Off
    • shrinks image back to default state
    • cursor returns to default state
    • left & right arrows ease out
    • page indicators ease out
    • drop shadow ease out
  • Click On
    • view exhibit details on another page

On View Box:

  • Hover On
    • a text called "This is available now" expands out towards the right with a duration of 800ms and bounces while it does that
  • Hover Off
    • the text shrinks back into the box

Page Indicators:

  • Hover On
    • page indicator becomes brighter slowly
  • Hover Off
    • page indicator returns to default
  • Click On
    • corresponding image to the indicator will replace the current image if need be
    • page indicator stays bright even after cursor hovers off
